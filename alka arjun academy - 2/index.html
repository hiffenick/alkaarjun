Our Story

ALKARJUN
SCHOOL OF
MARTIAL ART

Founded with a singular vision — to bring the true spirit of Karate to India — Alkarjun Academy has grown into Pune's most respected martial arts institution. Located in the heart of the city, we blend ancient tradition with a modern, high-performance approach.

Our state-of-the-art dojos feature traditional tatami mats, professional punching bags, and specialized equipment designed for every level. More importantly, our culture breeds champions — not just in competition, but in life.

Alkarjun is a community grounded in integrity, perseverance, and humility. We believe martial arts transforms lives — one kata, one belt, one breakthrough at a time.

The Journey

BELT
PROGRESSION

Every belt earned is a milestone of transformation — physical, mental, and philosophical.

01
White Belt
Purity and a fresh start — where every champion begins their journey.
02
Yellow Belt
First rays of sunlight — the student's awareness begins to grow.
03
Orange Belt
Energy and enthusiasm rise as training deepens and technique forms.
04
Green Belt
Skills take root and begin to grow — technique becomes natural.
05
Blue Belt
Stability and deeper understanding — the student's foundation solidifies.
06
Maroon Belt
Deep commitment and perseverance — readiness for advanced challenges.
07
Purple Belt
Transition from intermediate to advanced — technique meets artistry.
08
Red Belt
Intensity and power — the fiery spirit of an advanced practitioner.
09
Brown Belt
Refinement and readiness — preparation for the pinnacle begins.
10
Brown-Black Belt
Pre-mastery — the threshold between advanced student and instructor.
11
Black Belt
Not the end — but the beginning of true mastery and lifelong learning.
